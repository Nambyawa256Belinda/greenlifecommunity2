Who we are and what we stand for.

OUR BACKGROUND

Green Life Community is a community-focused environmental initiative created to encourage people to take practical steps toward a cleaner and healthier environment. The organization promotes conservation, responsible waste management, environmental education and community participation.

MISSION

To empower communities to protect the environment through education, sustainable practices, conservation and collective action.

VISION

A clean, green and environmentally responsible community where people and nature thrive together.

OUR VALUE

Sustainability •Community participation • Responsibility • Innovation • Environmental stewardship

WHAT WE DO

✓We plant trees in schools and communities

✓Teach households how to separate waste and we collect plastics for recycling

✓Monthly clean ups, school education and working with local communities
